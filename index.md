**What is a potential change you would make to the approach in your current Q1 Project?**

In the tone analysis part of my project, I think we could better define each tone in a quantitative way. We can experiment with giving the LLM several different prompts that fall under the same tone umbrella and see performance across each prompt. I also think the project would benefit from testing on a wider range of LLMs, including stronger ones like Claude. LLMs have become so good that stronger models may show much smaller differences between tones, but it's worth testing, and comparing how much each model is affected is interesting on its own. These are changes my partner and I are working on completing.
